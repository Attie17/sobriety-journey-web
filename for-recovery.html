Support for Recovery

Support for Your
Recovery Journey

Recovery does not end when treatment ends. Many people find that the months after treatment are when recovery habits must become part of everyday life. Sobriety Journey is designed to support this stage by helping you build structure, awareness, and stability in your daily life.

How it supports you

Stay aware. Build structure. Access support.

Five key ways the platform supports your recovery, every day.

📈

Stay aware of your recovery patterns

Daily check-ins help you notice changes in mood, cravings, sleep, and stress. Short, simple, and private.

📝

Reflect through journaling

Your private journal allows you to record thoughts, challenges, and progress. Your journal remains completely private.

🕐

Rebuild healthy routines

The routine builder helps you develop habits that support long-term recovery stability.

🔎

Recognise triggers earlier

Over time the app helps you understand situations that may increase vulnerability, before they become difficult to manage.

📞

Access support when you need it

Crisis tools and support contact options help you reach out when moments feel difficult. Grounding exercises and trusted contacts are always accessible.

Your Privacy

Your privacy matters.

Your journal and reflections remain private. The app supports your recovery journey but does not replace therapists, sponsors, or support groups. You remain in control of your experience.

Please note: AI-assisted reflections are supportive prompts only. They are not clinical assessments and do not replace professional support. Always speak with your therapist or counsellor about significant changes in your recovery.
